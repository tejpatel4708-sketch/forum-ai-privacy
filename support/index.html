Forum AI Support

Help with the Forum AI app for iPhone.

Contact us: support@wheremarketsthink.com

Include what you were doing and, if it helps, a screenshot. Please don't send anything you'd rather keep private.

Report a response

If an advisor said something wrong, unsafe, or inappropriate, tap Settings → Report a response in the app, or email us at the address above. Tell us roughly what was asked and what the advisor said.

Delete your data

In the app, open Settings → Delete all my data. This removes every conversation, decision, link, and preference from your iPhone and deletes your anonymous Forum account. To remove a single conversation, swipe it away in your conversation history.

Forum can't answer my question

Forum needs an internet connection, and it needs your agreement to send your questions to its AI provider. If you chose "Not now" on the first screen, open the app again and tap Review and agree. If you see a message that Forum has reached its capacity for today, please try again tomorrow.

About the advisors

Forum's advisors are AI. They can be wrong and don't know your whole situation. Forum is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, or mental-health advice.
